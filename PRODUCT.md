# Product

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Angelo composes designs beside a PRIXEL stamp kit and transfers them to physical setup plates. The build brief and hardware contract in docs/ are the source of product requirements. Success means a legible composition, honest inventory accounting, and correctly sized, mirrored setup sheets.

## Design principles

Make the plate the main workspace. Keep piece selection, pass order and validation visible together. Separate physical piece colours from selected ink colours. Preserve composition work through undo and portable files. State hardware assumptions wherever buildability is reported. Use the supplied vendor artwork.

## Interface direction

A quiet, light workbench suitable for working beside paper in daylight. Familiar controls and compact labelled tools. This is an implementation assumption from the build brief, not a separately confirmed brand identity. No marketing shell or decorative dashboard metrics.

## Accessibility

Provide keyboard placement, movement, rotation and deletion; labelled controls; visible focus; text alongside ink colours and validation states. Respect reduced motion. No additional user-specific accessibility needs were supplied.
